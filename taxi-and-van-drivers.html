Your vehicle is your income. Let's keep you working.

If you drive a taxi, private hire car or van for work, a non-fault accident costs you more than a repair bill. Here's what we can do.

What we can arrange

  • A like-for-like replacement for work. For taxis and private hire, that means a vehicle that can be licensed to work, where one is available in your area.
  • Fast recovery, so you're not stuck at the roadside losing fares.
  • Repairs at approved garages, with costs claimed from the at-fault insurer.
  • Lost earnings. If you can't get a suitable replacement, lost income may be claimable from the at-fault insurer. Keep your records.

What to have ready

  1. Your licence details (badge and plate numbers, and which council licensed you, if you're a taxi or private hire driver).
  2. Proof of earnings, such as app statements, booking records or accounts.
  3. The other driver's details, plus photos and any dashcam footage.

Your replacement taxi or private hire car usually has to be licensed by your council before you can work in it. Tell us where you're licensed, and we'll explain what's possible and how long it's likely to take.

Subject to eligibility and the other party being found at fault.
