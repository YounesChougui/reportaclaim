Hit while parked? You can still claim.

If another driver hit your car while it was parked and they can be identified, it's a non-fault claim. You shouldn't have to pay your excess or lose your no-claims bonus.

What to do right now

  1. Check for a note. A driver who damages a parked car must stop and leave their details, or report it to the police.
  2. Photograph everything. The damage close up and from further back, where the car was parked, and any paint transfer or debris.
  3. Look for cameras and witnesses. Ask nearby homes, shops and car parks about CCTV or doorbell cameras. Footage is often deleted within days, so ask quickly.
  4. Report it to the police if there's no note. Get a reference number. It helps if the driver is traced later.
  5. Tell us. We'll go through the evidence with you and explain your options.

No note and no witnesses?

If the driver can't be identified, the claim works differently. Read our hit and run guide before you do anything else.

What we can sort out

  • Recovery, if the car isn't safe to drive
  • A like-for-like replacement while yours is off the road
  • Repairs at an approved bodyshop
  • Dealing with the at-fault driver's insurer for you

Subject to eligibility and the other driver being identified and found at fault.
