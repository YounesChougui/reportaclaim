Hit by a driver who didn't stop?

It's frightening and it feels unfair. What happens next depends on whether the driver can be traced, so the first few hours matter.

Start your claim in about 30 seconds

Or call free on 0808 157 0420, 24/7

If anyone is hurt or the road is unsafe, call 999. Otherwise report the collision to the police as soon as you can, and keep the reference number.

What to do straight away

  1. Write down what you remember. Even part of the number plate, the colour, make or model, and which way they went.
  2. Report it to the police. Leaving the scene without giving details is an offence. A police report is usually needed for any claim.
  3. Find footage. Your dashcam, other drivers' dashcams, CCTV and doorbell cameras. Ask quickly, as recordings are often overwritten.
  4. Get witness details. A name and phone number is enough.

If the driver is traced

Once the driver and their insurer are identified, it's treated like any other non-fault claim. We can arrange recovery, a replacement vehicle and repairs, and claim the costs from their insurer.

If the driver is never found, or isn't insured

Claims involving uninsured or untraced drivers are usually handled by the Motor Insurers' Bureau (MIB). The MIB has its own rules and time limits, and cover for damage to your car is limited when the driver is never identified. Your own insurance policy may also help.
