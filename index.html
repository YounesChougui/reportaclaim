Straight answers.

Can't find yours? Call us. We'd rather explain on the phone than have you guess.

Will I have to pay an excess?+

Not on eligible non-fault claims. Because we claim your costs back from the at-fault driver's insurer, you don't pay your policy excess upfront.

Will this affect my no-claims bonus?+

We deal with the other driver's insurer directly, so the claim isn't made on your policy. Your no-claims bonus stays protected. You should still tell your own insurer an accident happened, because most policies require it.

What kind of replacement car will I get?+

A like-for-like vehicle, as close as possible to your own in size, type and specification. That includes automatics, people carriers and vans where you need them.

What if the other driver disputes liability?+

It happens. We gather the evidence (photos, witnesses, dashcam footage) and handle the dispute for you. We'll always be clear with you about where the claim stands.

Do I have to use a claims company?+

No. You can claim directly from the at-fault driver's insurer, or through your own insurer, at no cost. We handle the whole process for you, including recovery, a replacement vehicle and repairs, so you don't have to deal with it yourself.

Can I cancel?+

Yes. You have a 14-day cooling-off period after signing any agreement with us, and we'll explain your cancellation rights clearly before you commit to anything.
