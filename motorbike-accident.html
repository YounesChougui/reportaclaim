Knocked off your bike? We'll get you back riding.

When another driver causes the crash, you shouldn't be out of pocket or off the road for weeks. Here's what to do and what we can arrange.

Get checked over, even if you feel fine. Adrenaline hides injuries. If anyone is hurt or the road is unsafe, call 999.

At the scene

  1. Get yourself and the bike out of danger, if it's safe to move.
  2. Swap details with the other driver: name, address, phone, registration and insurer.
  3. Photograph the bike, the other vehicle, its plate, the road layout and your kit.
  4. Keep your damaged kit. Don't throw away a helmet, jacket or gloves. They're evidence, and damaged kit can often be included in the claim.

What we can arrange

  • Specialist motorcycle recovery, so your bike is transported properly
  • A replacement bike while yours is off the road, where one is available
  • Repairs at a bike specialist, or a fair valuation if it's written off
  • A referral to regulated specialists if you were injured

Subject to eligibility and the other party being found at fault.
